Full-Length Mock Exam

A timed, full-length simulation of the GRE General Test's Verbal and Quantitative sections — four sections, no breaks, no answer feedback until the whole exam is done.

What this does and doesn't simulate: matches ETS's current section structure and timing exactly — Verbal Section 1 (12 questions, 18 min), Verbal Section 2 (15 questions, 23 min), Quantitative Section 1 (12 questions, 21 min), Quantitative Section 2 (15 questions, 26 min), 1 hr 28 min total, sections in random order, no breaks. It leaves out the 30-minute Analytical Writing essay (nothing here can grade writing) and is not section-level adaptive — every section draws from a fixed mix of question types and difficulties rather than getting harder or easier based on how you did on the previous section. Your reported Verbal and Quant scores are an estimate on the official 130–170 scale, not an ETS-calculated score — ETS doesn't publish its real scoring formula.
